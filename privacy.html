Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how alexco.dev and edu.alexco.dev ("we", "our", "us") collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our websites.

Data Collection

We use Google Analytics to track site performance and user interactions. This helps us understand how our websites are used and improve the user experience.

Information Collected

Google Analytics may collect the following data:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring websites
  • Device and browser information
  • General geographic location (city-level)

This data is anonymized and does not include personally identifiable information (PII).

Data Usage

The collected data is used solely for:

  • Analyzing website performance
  • Improving content and user experience
  • Identifying technical issues

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute collected data to third parties. All data remains within our analytics system for internal use only.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to track interactions. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer not to be tracked.

Your Choices

If you do not want your data collected, you can:

  • Disable cookies in your browser
  • Use browser extensions that block tracking scripts
  • Opt out of Google Analytics tracking via Google's opt-out tool

Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact alex@alexco.dev.
